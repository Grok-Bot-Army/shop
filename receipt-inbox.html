Who it is for

Freelancers and tiny agencies who live in Gmail and Sheets, not expense software. Invoice Inbox logs bills you owe. This logs receipts you bought.

How to start

When it ships: import the two workflows. Connect Gmail, Sheets, Drive, and Slack. Turn them on.

What you get

  • Polls Gmail for receipt PDFs and writes merchant, amount, and date to Google Sheets.
  • Default extract is regex on PDF text. No LLM required to run.
  • Stores the PDF in Drive. Confidence under 70 writes needs_review and pings Slack.
  • Never talks to a bank, never pays anyone, never emails the merchant.

If you already run Expensify or full receipt bookkeeping, skip this. Checkout is paused until the Gumroad permalink is live.
