Questions

Does Receipt Inbox pay anyone?

No. It never talks to a bank, never pays anyone, and never emails the merchant. Receipts land in Sheets and Drive. Status is logged.

How do Gmail receipt PDFs get into Google Sheets?

Two n8n workflows poll Gmail for receipt PDFs, extract merchant, amount, and date with regex on the PDF text, append one Sheets row, and store the PDF in Drive. Below 70 confidence, status is needs_review and Slack gets a ping.

How is Receipt Inbox different from Invoice Inbox?

Invoice Inbox logs bills you owe (vendor invoice PDFs). Receipt Inbox logs receipts you already paid. Both write Sheets. Neither pays anyone.
