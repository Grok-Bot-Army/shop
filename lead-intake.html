What it does

Lead Intake is n8n lead intake. One POST webhook. It flattens Typeform, Tally, Fillout, or a random webhook into one canonical lead row in Google Sheets. HubSpot or Pipedrive if you want them.

That is the whole job: form webhook flatten to one lead row. Sheets first. Nobody gets a surprise email.

Supported sources

Four payload shapes. Point Tally, Typeform, Fillout, or generic JSON at the same webhook.

  • Typeform to Google Sheets — same POST, one flattened lead row.
  • Tally form webhook to Sheets — same flatten, same sheet.
  • Fillout to Sheets — same webhook, same canonical row.
  • A random webhook / generic JSON — if it is not one of those three, it still goes through the flatten.

What it never does

It never emails the lead. There is no welcome sequence, no autoresponder, no “thanks for submitting” in this pack. If you want the workflow to email the lead, skip this.

Not for Salesforce, Facebook Lead Ads, or anyone who wants the workflow to email the lead.

Who it is for

Solo operators with more than one form and a spreadsheet they do not trust. You already run Tally, Typeform, Fillout, or a random webhook. You want one Sheets row you can actually use.
